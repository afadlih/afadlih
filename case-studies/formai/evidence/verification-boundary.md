# FormAI — Verification Boundary

## Supported public claims

- The project is designed around analyze, map, validate, preview, execute, and diagnose stages.
- Explicit data sources take precedence over AI fallback.
- The workflow distinguishes direct HTTP and browser-based execution paths.
- Limitations and failure states are documented rather than hidden.

## Not claimed

- Public source availability or independent reproduction.
- Compatibility with every Google Form structure.
- Production throughput, success-rate, or adoption metrics.
- Permission to expose real forms, respondent records, credentials, or execution logs.

The public material is a sanitized engineering case study. Stronger evidence may be added only after it is tied to a revision and reviewed for privacy.
